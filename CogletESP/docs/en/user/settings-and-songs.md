# Settings and songs

[Documentation home](../../README.md) · **English** · [Deutsch](../../de/user/settings-and-songs.md) · [Español](../../es/user/settings-and-songs.md) · [日本語](../../ja/user/settings-and-songs.md)

Both are on the [Coglet Updater](https://will-cogley.github.io/Coglet/) page.
Connect Coglet as for an update (top USB-C port, mode switch on **ESP**).
Settings and songs are kept through every update; only a **Fresh install**
erases them.

## Settings

Click **Show Coglet's settings**, change what you like, then **Save to Coglet**.

| Setting | Values |
|---|---|
| Wake word | English words, e.g. `hey coglet` or `hello robot` (3 to 40 letters; spaces are fine). Two or three clearly different words work best |
| Wake-up sensitivity | 1 to 50. Lower numbers wake up more easily, but also by mistake more often. Try 2 if Coglet often misses you, 10 if it wakes up by itself |
| Startup sound | Mecha, Evil, Cat, Christmas, Elf, Snowman |
| Reminder sound | Mecha, Evil, Cat, Christmas, Elf, Snowman |
| Talk over Coglet | On: Coglet keeps listening while it speaks, so you can interrupt it without the wake word |

- If Coglet can't use a wake word (an unusual spelling), it keeps its normal
  wake word, and the page tells you.
- Changing **Talk over Coglet** restarts Coglet (about 20 seconds). It works
  best with the volume at 50 % or lower.
- **Use defaults** and then **Save to Coglet** returns everything to the
  firmware's own defaults. Each edition has its own default sounds.

## Your own songs

Coglet can dance to your music. The page converts the songs in your browser;
nothing is uploaded to the internet.

1. Under **My songs**, choose audio or video files, or drop them on
   **Add songs** (mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg, mp4, ...).
2. Check the name and the dance style for each song:
   - **energetic**: the whole robot dances, with big, fast moves;
   - **calm**: ears, head and base sway gently on the slow beat; the eyes rest;
   - **automatic**: the page picks one.
3. Click **Save to Coglet**.
4. Say "dance to one of my songs" or "dance to *song name*".

- There is room for about 8 one-minute songs; the bar shows how much is left.
  Songs are cut to 60 seconds with a fade-out.
- **Show songs on Coglet** lists the stored songs, so you can remove some.
- If a song dances oddly, change its style and save it again.
- Only use music you have the rights to.

Songs need Chrome or Edge 94 or newer.
