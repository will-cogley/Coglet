Update Coglet

  1. Plug Coglet into this computer through the top USB-C port, with a data cable (some cables only charge).
  2. Leave the small switch on the board on ESP, as it comes from the factory.
  3. Click Connect & Update and choose Coglet in the list that appears.
  4. Wait until it says Done (1 to 3 minutes). Keep Coglet plugged in meanwhile.

Built your own firmware? (for example with songs in the Music folder, or changed settings) This installs the official version instead; rebuild yours afterwards. Songs added under My songs on this page are always kept.

My songs

    
Add your own music and ask Coglet to dance to it, for example "dance to Night Rain" or "dance to one of my songs". Coglet finds the beat by itself and picks a dance style: energetic (big moves, eyes join in) or calm (gentle sway). The songs are stored on Coglet and kept through updates.

    
Up to 60 seconds per song (longer songs fade out). Most audio and video files work (mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg, mp4…). Please only add music you have the right to use.

      Questions and problems
      
    
      Coglet isn't in the list of ports
      
      Try another USB cable (many cables only charge), another USB port on the computer, and make sure you use Coglet's top USB-C port with the switch on ESP. On Windows the USB driver installs by itself the first time; give it a minute. If it still doesn't show up, tick Show all ports under Advanced options.
      
    
      There are several ports in the list. Which one is Coglet?
      
      Unplug Coglet and connect again: the entry that disappeared is Coglet. Usually it's called "USB Serial", "CH340/CH343", "CP210x" or "USB JTAG/serial". The page remembers your choice.
      
    
      "Failed to connect"
      
      Close anything else that may be using the port (Thonny, the Arduino IDE, another tab with this page), then try again. If it still fails, hold the BOOT button, press and release RESET, release BOOT, and click Connect & Update. A slower upload speed under Advanced options can help too.
      
    
      What if the update stops halfway, or I unplug Coglet by accident?
      
      Nothing breaks for good: plug Coglet back in and run the update again. If the servo board was being updated when the power went off, Coglet finishes or repeats that by itself the next time it starts.
      
    
      Do I have to open Coglet to reach the switch?
      
      Only for the one-time servo board setup, and only on Coglets made before November 2026. Normal updates use the ESP position, as Coglet comes. If you'd rather not open it, you can still update: everything works except new servo moves, which need that one step.
      
    
      What's new in this version?
      
      See the release notes on GitHub.
      
    
      I build my own firmware. Will this page overwrite it?
      
      Yes: Connect & Update installs the official firmware. Songs in your Music folder are compiled into your own build, so they are replaced too; the files on your computer are not touched, and rebuilding brings them back. Songs added on this page are stored separately and kept. To keep your own changes, merge the new version into your code and build it yourself.
      
    
      How many songs fit, and why does a song dance oddly?
      
      About 8 one-minute songs (2 MB); the bar above shows the room left. If the moves feel wrong for a song, change its style to energetic or calm before saving: calm songs are danced on the slow, felt beat.
      
    
      After updating, Coglet says a 6-digit code
      
      Coglet needs to be paired with xiaozhi.me again. This only happens after a fresh install.
      
    
      The servo board setup can't connect
      
      Make sure the switch is on RP2040 and that nothing else (Thonny, another tab) has the port open. If the board never had MicroPython installed, ask in the community for the starter firmware first.
      
    
      Asking for help
      
      Click Show device log, make the problem happen, then click Copy log and post it in the community. After an update the log opens by itself.
